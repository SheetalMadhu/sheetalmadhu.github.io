Work

Projects I'm proud of.

AI · Medical Imaging · Published

Neonatal Ocular Analytics (NOA): ROP Staging & Prognostication

An AI-powered system for detecting and staging Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) in premature infants from fundus retinal images. Evaluated 7 deep learning architectures on a dataset of 3,147 augmented fundus images. The best model, AlexNet, outperformed a published three-stream architecture combining ResNet18, DenseNet121, and EfficientNetB2.

98.71% AlexNet accuracy
99.85% validation accuracy
Python TensorFlow Keras OpenCV HTML/CSS/JS MySQL
Published · IJNRD Vol. 10, Issue 7, July 2025
Computer Vision · Smart Cities · Published

Smart Bin: AI-Powered Waste Classification

A YOLOv5-based waste detection system that classifies waste as dry or wet through a web-based image upload interface. Designed to assist urban waste management by automating the sorting process and reducing manual effort. Outperformed the existing benchmark model on glass and metal detection.

85% Glass detection accuracy
vs 70% in benchmark model
Python YOLOv5 OpenCV HTML/CSS/JS
Published · IJNRD Vol. 9, Issue 7, July 2024
